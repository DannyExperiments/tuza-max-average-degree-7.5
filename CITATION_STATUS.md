### Description for the archive

Let ν(G) be the maximum number of edge-disjoint triangles in a finite simple graph G and τ(G) the minimum number of edges meeting every triangle. We prove τ(G) ≤ 2ν(G) whenever the maximum average degree is at most 7.5. The strict bound uses Kandasamy’s Lemma 3.5, and equality also uses his Theorem 1.1. We prove the same inequality when the vertices of degree at least eight induce a triangle-free subgraph. Every counterexample must contain two fans on vertices of degree at least eight whose seven-edge sets are distinct. The fans may overlap. An open four-edge condition would suffice for the unrestricted conjecture. The included census through ten vertices confirms an earlier computation. The manuscript is internally reviewed, not peer reviewed.

The human project lead selected the problem, proposed rotating three-dimensional graph diagrams and requested independent checks. The [archive disclosure](AI_DISCLOSURE.md#disclosure-for-the-archive) supplies the remainder of the record description and identifies each system’s work.
