# Claims and evidence

“Proved” here records an internally reviewed mathematical proof with the stated inputs. “Computational” specifies a finite enumeration or certificate replay. “Conditional” specifies an implication. “Open” identifies an unproved universal statement. Historical priority and independent publication are separate questions.

| Claim | Status | Dependencies and check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Every finite simple $G$ with $\mathrm{mad}(G)<15/2$ satisfies $\tau\le 2\nu$. | Proved | Local reductions of Puleo and Botler–Fernandes–Gutiérrez, the seven-link and adjacent-seven certificates extending Gupta, local octahedral-six input from Kandasamy Lemma 3.5 with connected link, donor bounds, eligible-recipient lemma, and the $1/30$ transfer. [Proof](canonical/density-endpoint.md) · [Donor arithmetic](verification/check_count.py) · [Endpoint and transfer arithmetic](verification/check_endpoint.py) |
| A2. The same conclusion holds when $\mathrm{mad}(G)=15/2$. | Proved | A1’s charge calculation is strict in a minimal violation containing a vertex of degree at least eleven. Kandasamy Theorem 1.1 gives such a vertex. The endpoint depends on that preprint and on A1’s local inputs. [Manuscript](paper/manuscript.pdf) · [Kandasamy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15269) |
| B. If the vertices of degree at least eight induce a triangle-free graph, the whole graph satisfies $\tau\le2\nu$. | Proved | Direct proof uses the five-wide-spokes lemma for sevens, pair reductions at codegrees 4, 5, 6, low-degree reductions, and the packing on a cubic component joined to a square. Also follows from C. [Proof](canonical/triangle-free-high.md) · [Adjacency certificates](verification/verify_pairs.py) |
| C. Every violating graph has at least two distinct seven-edge high fans $K_1\vee P_4$. | Proved | Heredity of the at-most-one-fan hypothesis, complete local link markings, tetrahedron reductions, the two-exception cycle payment and forest count. The copies can overlap on all five vertices. [Proof](canonical/two-fans.md) · [High-link audit](verification/four_high_audit.py) · [Two-fan check](verification/check_two_fans.py) |
| Finite inputs for A–C | Computational | The codegree-four pair census has 3,286 classes. Universal certificates for 3,283 were checked on all 16 completions each (52,528). Three exceptional classes are treated structurally. Exact codegree-five and six reductions and the Gupta certificate input have separate verifiers. [Pair verification](verification/verify_pairs.py) · [Gupta input](verification/check_gupta.py) · [Codegrees five and six](verification/check_gupta_56.py) |
| If the four-edge simultaneous-avoidance assertion always holds, Tuza holds for every finite simple graph. | Conditional | Least-violation reduction, localization to a tight triangle component, minimum blocked two-edge path, and two returning pages. [Exact hypotheses](PROBLEM_AND_PROOF.md#further-results) |
| The four-edge simultaneous-avoidance assertion always holds; Tuza holds without a restriction. | Open | Separate maximum packings avoiding the two halves need not coincide by definition. No universal joint packing or unrestricted inequality is established here. |
| Through order ten the only positive tight triangle-connected supports are $K_4$ and $K_5$. | Computational | Exact support and coverage tests, finite order bound, complete parent catalogues, and the documented replay. Confirms the July census. Order ten is a manual replay. [Protocol and counts](REPRODUCIBILITY.md) |

The integral packing and cover throughout concern all ambient triangles. No maximum-degree or edge-codegree bound is assumed in A. Published comparison points include Puleo’s $\mathrm{mad}<7$, the maximum-degree results of Gupta and Kandasamy, and Tsujimura’s overlap-restricted density results. Puleo’s link analysis already supplies high fans in the all-high minimal case. Historical priority of the full mixed case and of A has not been established.
